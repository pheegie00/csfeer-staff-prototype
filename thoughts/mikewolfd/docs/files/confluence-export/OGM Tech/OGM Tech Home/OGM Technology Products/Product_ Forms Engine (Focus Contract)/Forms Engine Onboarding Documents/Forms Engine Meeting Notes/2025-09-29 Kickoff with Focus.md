**Relevant Language from PWS (on the Kickoff Meeting)**

Within five days of contract award, the Contractor shall coordinate with the COR to schedule the   
kick-off meeting. Meeting coordination including a draft agenda and suggested list of questions   
that will require decisions by the COR should be conducted through the  
[ACFOCIOContractStatusReport@acf.hhs.gov](mailto:ACFOCIOContractStatusReport@acf.hhs.gov) mailbox. The resulting meeting invite should be   
addressed to the CO, COR, representatives from the OCIO Portfolio Management and Governance   
(PMG) Division, and representatives from the respective Program Office leadership. The meeting   
shall be conducted either virtually or at the designated government offices, to review project goals,   
tasks, timelines, and deliverables. At least one (1) business day prior to the Kick-off Meeting, the   
Contractor shall prepare an agenda indicating who will attend the meeting on behalf of the   
Contractor and provide a list of questions that will require decisions by the COR at the time of the   
Kick-off Meeting. Included in this meeting will be a detailed discussion of plans for all management   
and reporting deliverables, including work plans and management calls, as well as details for the   
agenda for technical kick-off meeting. The Contractor shall prepare the kick-off meeting minutes 3   
days after kickoff meeting.   
  
During the kick-off meeting, the Contractor shall address/provide a draft schedule, transition-in   
approach (if required), initial risk register, discuss compliance with the ACF OCIO Governance   
Framework (initial tailoring plan), performance metrics reporting, initial project staffing with   
approach for requesting access to/ensuring compliance with ACF/government systems, and   
approach for meeting support (agendas, minutes, etc.). The Contractor shall complete a ACF OCIO   
Intake Request Form within 5 days of award and submit to the ACF OCIO Portfolio Management   
and Governance Division, [Customer\_Engagement@acf.hhs.gov](mailto:Customer_Engagement@acf.hhs.gov) to formalize/start the ACF OCIO   
Governance Framework requirements.
