| [CSBG IM #152 Annual Report](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/policy-guidance/csbg-im-152-annual-report)  The CSBG Annual Report is organized in four modules. <br/><ul><li><strong>Module 1: State Administration</strong><span> </span>(completed by State CSBG Administrators) includes information on State administration of CSBG funding, including information on distribution of funds to eligible entities, use of State administrative funds and discretionary funds for training and technical assistance, as well as information on eligible entity organizational standards progress, and the State’s progress meeting accountability measures related to State monitoring, training and technical assistance and other critical areas.</li></ul><ul><li><strong>Module 2: Agency Expenditures, Capacity, and Resources<span> </span></strong>(completed by eligible entities; reviewed, evaluated, and analyzed by State CSBG Lead Agencies) includes information on funds spent by eligible entities on the direct delivery of local services and strategies and capacity development as well as information on funding devoted to administrative costs by the eligible entities.</li></ul><ul><li><strong>Module 3: Community Level</strong><span> </span>(completed by eligible entities; reviewed, evaluated, and analyzed by State CSBG Lead Agencies) includes information on the implementation and results achieved for community-level strategies.</li></ul><ul><li><strong>Module 4: Individual and Family Level</strong><span> </span>(completed by eligible entities; reviewed, evaluated; analyzed by State CSBG Lead Agencies) includes information on services provided to individuals and families, demographic characteristics of people served by eligible entities, and the results of these services.</li></ul> There's also the CSBG CARES Annual Report for supplemental funding. <br/>[CSBG Annual Report Submission FY 2023](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/policy-guidance/csbg-annual-report-submission-fy-2023)*Pre-Populated Information and CSBG Eligible Entities* As this year’s CSBG Annual Report submission is for FY2023 activities, the CSBG-eligible entity information are based on the information from the Federal Fiscal Year (FFY) 2023 CSBG State Plan and are **pre-populated in OLDC.** <br/>*Webinars**Communications* Please verify whether you are on the CSBG mailing list, and if not send an email to **[CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov](mailto:CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov)**. OCS created this new resource mailbox as a dedicated location for responding to and tracking technical assistance requests, ex. mailing list updates, website contacts updates, and OLDC access and reporting issues. <br/>*SmartForms and OLDC* OLDC will open for all CSBG grant recipients no later than **November 2, 2023**. <br/> OCS will release **SmartForms** for all CSBG grant recipients with a completed FY2022 CSBG Annual Report no later than **November 27, 2023**. <br/>*OLDC Access* OCS encourages all grant recipients to review OLDC access information and ensure correct assignments for all staff for the CSBG Annual Report submission process. Each state should have at least one individual assigned as the Grant Administrator. OCS does not require the Authorized Official to certify the CSBG Annual Report; therefore, the Grant Administrator is able to certify. <br/> There is a new process for requesting accounts for GrantSolutions and OLDC. Please complete the [**Recipient User Account Request Form**](https://gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fhome.grantsolutions.gov%2Fhome%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2022%2F05%2FGrant-Recipient-User-Account-Request-Form-5.2022-UEI_508.pdf&data=05%7C01%7CNikita.Frazier%40ACF.hhs.gov%7Ce8ea5c0eb27f45da5dcb08dacbde32a6%7Cd58addea50534a808499ba4d944910df%7C0%7C0%7C638046452431786454%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=Z34JZox9A3%2BL6ZpgTE4rxreb82gKDLQadI5GwKQaias%3D&reserved=0)and the [**OLDC Access Form**](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/documents/ocs/oldc_access_form_0.docx) (DOCX), and submit both to the [**GrantSolutions Helpdesk**](mailto:help@grantsolutions.gov) to create the account. <br/> If you have questions regarding OLDC access or submissions, please send an email to **[CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov](mailto:CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov)**. <br/>*Support* Office Hours <br/> If you have any questions regarding this communication or your CSBG Annual Reports, please contact your [**assigned Program Specialist**](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/contact-information/csbg-contact-info-staff-assignments-region). We ask that you copy **[CSBGStates@acf.hhs.gov](mailto:CSBGStates@acf.hhs.gov)**ttto ensure an expedited response.  <br/>[Tribal Annual Report Submission FY24](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/policy-guidance/acf-ocs-csbg-25-02-tribal-annual-report-submission-fy24)*New for FY24 Tribal Annual Report Submissions* CSBG direct-funded tribal grant recipients are required to complete **either** the Tribal Annual Report or Tribal Annual Report (Short Form). The CSBG Tribal Annual Report is required for CSBG direct-funded grant recipients receiving $50,000 or more in annual funding. The Short Form version of the CSBG Tribal Annual Report is required for CSBG direct-funded tribes and tribal organizations receiving less than $50,000 in annual funding. The aim of offering two reports by funding amount is to reduce burden in an equitable manner for tribes and tribal organizations receiving CSBG funding. The attached allocation table indicates funding levels to ensure tribes and tribal organizations submit the appropriate annual report. <br/> This CSBG Annual Report using the Tribal Annual Report or Tribal Annual Report (Short Form) covers activities completed during FY24 (October 1, 2023 - September 30, 2024). The deadline for all CSBG grant recipients to submit their required and **complete**FY2024 CSBG Annual Report is March 31, 2025. A complete submission includes: <br/><ul><li><span lang="EN">Tribal Administration (Module 1) </span></li><li><span lang="EN">Tribal CSBG Expenditures (Module 2)<strong> </strong></span></li><li><span lang="EN">Individual and Family (Module 3)<strong> </strong></span></li></ul>*Tools* CSBG direct-funded tribes and tribal organizations have tools available to ensure a complete and timely submission to include fillable forms and the Online Data Collection system.  <br/> All CSBG Tribal Annual Report materials and information can be found in the CSBG Tribal Annual Report Toolkit including the following: <br/><ul><li><span lang="EN">FY24 CSBG Tribal Annual Report Tool (tribal grant recipients receiving $50,000 or more in annual funding)</span></li><li><span lang="EN">FY24 CSBG Tribal Annual Report (Short Form) Tool (tribal grant recipients receiving less than $50,000 in annual funding)</span></li><li><span lang="EN">CSBG Tribal Annual Report OLDC Submission Step-by-Step instructions</span></li><li><span lang="EN">Reporting by Allocation List</span></li></ul> OCS encourages all tribal grant recipients to save their content in the fillable tool and review submission prior to attaching the form in OLDC. It is important to follow [**OLDC Step-by-Step Instructions**](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/documents/ocs/TTA_CSBG%20Tribal%20Annual%20Report%20OLDC%20Instructions.pdf) (PDF)to complete the applicable Tribal Annual Report form and attach the completed form in OLDC. <br/> If the tribe or tribal organization requires any staff member to obtain access to OLDC, complete both the [**Recipient User Account Request Form**](https://home.grantsolutions.gov/home/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Grant-Recipient-User-Account-Request-Form-revised-05-01-24.pdf) (PDF) and the [**OLDC Access Form**](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/documents/ocs/CSBG-Tribal-Annual-Report-OLDC-Instructions_1.pdf) (PDF) and submit the forms to the [**GrantSolutions Helpdesk**](mailto:help@grantsolutions.gov) to request a new account. If you have questions regarding OLDC access, please send an email to [**CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov**](mailto:CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov) <br/>*Technical Assistance* In addition to the webinars, OCS is pleased to offer two additional methods for targeted training and technical assistance (TTA): OCS Annual Report Office Hours and OCS Targeted TTA referrals. <br/> OCS will host office hours to support tribes and tribal organizations in submitting their annual reports on: <br/><ul><li><span lang="EN">Tuesday, February 11, 2025, 3:00 - 4:00 p.m. ET</span></li></ul> Tribes and tribal organizations will receive additional information on registration for Annual Report Office Hours in January 2025.  <br/> As part of OCS commitment to providing [effective training and technical assistance](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/policy-guidance/acf-ocs-csbg-dcl-24-10-effective-training-and-technical-assistance-commitment), tribes and tribal organizations may request targeted TTA with an OCS-funded TTA provider available at no-cost by [submitting a request](mailto:CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov?subject=Request%20for%20Targeted%20TTA) to their assigned [Data and Evaluation Specialist](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/contact-information/csbg-contact-info-staff-assignments-region) and copying [CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov](mailto:CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov). <br/>*Resources and Support* If you have any questions regarding this communication or your CSBG Annual Report, please contact your [**assigned Data and Evaluation Specialist**](mailto:assigned%20Data%20and%20Evaluation%20Specialist). We ask that you copy [CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov](mailto:CSBGData@acf.hhs.gov) to ensure a timely response. OCS encourages grant recipients to use the CSBG Tribal Report Toolkit to support successful submissions. <br/> Submitting the FY24 CSBG Annual Report by March 31, 2025, ensures grant recipient compliance as outlined in Section 678(b) of the CSBG Act, and helps describe the effectiveness and outcomes of the CSBG Network to be included in the FY24 OCS Annual Report.   <br/> Thank you for your attention to these matters.
