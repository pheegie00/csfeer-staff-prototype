### Tribal Annual Report

There are two forms the plan (before the money) and the report (after the money). There are technically two different types of reports. We have one for Tribes and Tribal Organizations (T/TO) receiving more than $50k and one for those receiving less than $50k. Providing some basic info below:

- Tribal Plan (user count ~66)
- Tribal Short Form (user count ~30 but could be up to 66 as a safeguard)
- Tribal Annual Report (user count ~30 but could be up to 66 as a safeguard)
