# Strategy Artifacts

This work helps us understand the current experience and map the larger context around the existing CSBG forms. These artifacts will map out the larger landscape of systems and internal workflows to be considered as the project scales. These artifacts will be long-reaching in their goal and will continue to evolve as we learn more by engaging with internal staff and users/grantees.

#### Service Blueprint artifacts include:

- [Ecosystem Mapping](https://www.figma.com/board/zoPjGarJQBVwlMaIgISvq0/ACF-Exploration?node-id=6-138) - Includes departments, divisions and key individuals at CSBG and ACF at large, helps orient the team
- [Service Blueprint V0](https://www.figma.com/board/zoPjGarJQBVwlMaIgISvq0/ACF-Exploration?node-id=178-2&t=s1qUEhB2LCkqYlO8-1) - Scoped to Tribal Form submission workflows, this version provides an initial scaffolding of capturing phases and actions by staff and users/grantees

##### Evaluative Research artifacts include:

- [Research Plan](https://confluence.acf.gov/spaces/OXT/pages/168833918/Research+Plan) (WIP) for testing out designs with grant recipients - This lays out the plan for how we want to approach getting feedback from users on the forms and also includes the script we will use on the day of when testing with grant recipients/participants.

# Design Artifacts

This work is focused on the MVP deliverables for year 1, which include digitizing the Tribal Forms.[^

]
Research is embedded in this work to help us understand the current state and inform design artifacts.

##### Tribal Annual Report designs include:

- [User flow digram](https://www.figma.com/board/zoPjGarJQBVwlMaIgISvq0/ACF-Exploration?node-id=309-1975) for Tribal Annual Report - Getting familiarized with the form and help map out the sequence of steps to complete the form, also functions as an initial view of what the digital experience may look like
- [V1 clickable designs](https://www.figma.com/proto/wFHt90xC26RDWyIwNPtlua/Tribal-Forms?page-id=37%3A1969&node-id=105-37342&viewport=-130%2C621%2C0.11&t=jm9hEoRQxxmqcFHg-1&scaling=min-zoom&content-scaling=fixed&starting-point-node-id=105%3A37342) for Tribal Annual Report - This is a prototype of the first iteration of the Tribal Annual Report form. You can click through it to move between pages. It is meant to give a sense of what the final implementation could look like. Please note that the implementation version will vary based on feasibility discussions and how the USWDS components are set up to work together on a page.
- [V1.3 clickable designs](https://www.figma.com/proto/wFHt90xC26RDWyIwNPtlua/Tribal-Forms?page-id=1380%3A12305&node-id=1380-12685&viewport=2161%2C3778%2C0.2&t=v6fbePUbTWf3piQ8-1&scaling=min-zoom&content-scaling=fixed&starting-point-node-id=1380%3A12685) for Tribal Annual Report - This is a prototype of the most recent iteration of the form.
