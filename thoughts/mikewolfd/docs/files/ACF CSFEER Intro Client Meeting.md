## **Project Background & Goals**

* **Current Challenge:** OCS uses outdated (2015), fragmented technology for CSBG data collection, requiring extensive manual effort (9-12 months validation) and training for grantees.  
* **Core Problem:** Inefficient, form-based data processes that hinder timely, accurate reporting and performance management.  
* **Vision:** Build a unified, user-friendly system for pre-award planning, post-award reporting, and data validation to serve CSBG and potentially all OCS programs.  
* **Broader Impact:** Solution may be scaled to other OCS programs (e.g., LIHEAP, Diaper Program, Rural Development) and inform agency-wide improvements.

## **Success Metrics & Approach**

* **Functional Success:** System must meet legal data collection requirements, streamline workflows, and integrate with existing ACF/OCS systems.  
* **User Experience:** Intuitive forms, reduced complexity, regular engagement with grantees (especially tribal organizations), and ongoing usability testing.  
* **Scalability:** Solution should be adaptable for other OCS programs facing similar challenges.  
* **Trust & Collaboration:** Build strong relationships with stakeholders and maintain transparency throughout development.
